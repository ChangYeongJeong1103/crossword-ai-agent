# Puzzle attribution

The seven standard 15×15 fixtures are adapted from Crossword Nexus:

- “What A Fool Believes” — Alex Boisvert, © 2021.
- “I'm Alright” — Alex Boisvert, © 2022.
- “Gino (The Manager)” — Alex Boisvert, © 2022.
- “Garden Party” — Alex Boisvert, © 2022.
- “By George!” — Frisco and Alex Boisvert, © 2022.
- “Jesus Children of America” — Jayne and Alex Boisvert, © 2022.
- “The Cocoanuts” — Alex Boisvert, © 2022.

The source fixtures are distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Original page links, download links, dates, formats, and source SHA-256 values are retained in `data/benchmark/standard_15x15/selection_manifest.json` and `data/benchmark/held_out/selection_manifest.json`. They were converted from JPZ/iPuz into blank grids and clue lists, with solutions in separate gold files. The source hash covers the original source, not the converted JSON.

The design process referenced “Language Models are Crossword Solvers” (Saha et al., NAACL 2025): https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.104/

The heuristic controller is independently written and is not a SweepClip reproduction. No third-party few-shot support dataset is included or used.
